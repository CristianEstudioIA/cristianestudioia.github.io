True stories history forgot to tell you.

The Untold Archive is a YouTube channel of narrated history documentaries: mysteries at sea, lost expeditions, forgotten disasters and the strange people behind them. Every story is researched from documented sources and illustrated with public-domain and openly licensed historical images.

Watch on YouTube

How our videos are made

Each documentary starts from a documented historical source. Our team selects the story, writes an original script based only on that source, and pairs it with historical paintings, engravings, photographs and newspaper pages from Wikimedia Commons and The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access collection, with full credits in every video description.

Narration is produced with an AI voice, and this is disclosed on every video using YouTube's altered or synthetic content label.

The Untold Archive Publisher (our YouTube API client)

To publish and measure our own videos, we use a private internal tool, The Untold Archive Publisher, which connects to the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API using Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0). It is used only by the channel owner, only for the channel The Untold Archive, and it:

  • uploads our own finished videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to our channel;
  • reads public statistics about history videos to understand what topics audiences enjoy;
  • reads our own channel's analytics (views, watch time) to improve future videos.

It is not offered to other users and does not access any other person's account or private data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
