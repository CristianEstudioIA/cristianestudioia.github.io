Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how The Untold Archive ("we") handles information in connection with our website and our internal tool The Untold Archive Publisher (the "App"), which uses YouTube API Services.

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Untold Archive to manage that channel. It is not available to the public and no other users sign in to it.

2. Information the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload – to upload our own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to our channel;
  • youtube.readonly – to read our channel's videos and public information about other public YouTube videos (titles, view counts, channel subscriber counts) for topic research;
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read our own channel's analytics reports (views, watch time);
  • youtube.force-ssl – to add subtitles (captions) to our own videos and to organise our own videos into playlists on our channel.

The App does not access any other person's account, private videos, messages, contacts or personal data.

3. How the information is used

Data is used only to publish our videos and to produce internal reports that help us choose topics and improve our videos. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer YouTube data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Aggregated public statistics (titles and view counts of public videos) may be summarised by an AI text model to produce internal topic reports; no personal data is included.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and retention

The OAuth token and the reports are stored only on the channel owner's computer. Reports containing statistics obtained from YouTube API Services are automatically deleted after 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

5. YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.

7. Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information.
